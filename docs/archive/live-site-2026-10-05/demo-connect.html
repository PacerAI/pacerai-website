Add the connector 2 min

  1. Browser (recommended): open claude.ai/new?modal=add-custom-connector — it opens the Add-custom-connector dialog directly.
  2. Mobile or Desktop app: tap the + in the prompt area → Connectors → Manage connectors → Add custom connector.
  3. Enter the name Pacer AI Demo and this URL: https://pacerai-demo-mcp.azurewebsites.net/mcp
  4. Keep the detected defaults (Sign in now + Register automatically) and click Add.
  5. On the Pacer AI Demo sign-in page, enter your work email + the access password your Pacer contact shared with you.

Then just type

pace
gap
how do I close the gap to target?
Charts render right in the chat on Claude web/desktop. Follow up in plain English — ask about a segment, an account, or the month.
