Challenges

What to expect if you try a DIY approach

⏳

Time — 6+ months just to build the data cube

Source decisions, level of granularity, period reporting, static vs. streaming — each decision compounds the timeline before a single waterfall row is written.

👥

Team Size — 6+ members, including rare ARR expertise

You need a Financial Analyst, Data Engineer, Analytics Engineer, and at least one person with deep ARR modelling experience. That combination is hard to staff and expensive to retain.

📋

Output — a table is not a board report

Displaying numbers is one thing. Addressing ARR drivers by cohort and translating them into business implications for a board or acquirer is an entirely different skill set.

🤝

Non-Operational — insights never impact sales

Financial and data-mining insights sit in dashboards. They rarely reach the sales floor in a form reps can act on — territory planning, renewal risk, upsell prioritization.

🎯

Deep Expertise — hierarchy, architecture, presentation

An expert is needed to guide decisions on data hierarchy, bridge definitions, presentation methods, operational requirements, and technical architecture. Most teams are making these calls without one.

🔭

New lens — a new way of seeing your business, with no guide

ARR Snowball analysis changes how leadership understands the business. But without someone to lead that transformation, the insights never land — and the investment in the model goes unrealized.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What size companies do you work with? +

We focus on PE-backed B2B SaaS companies between $50M and $1B ARR. This is where the complexity of ARR reporting meets the scrutiny of institutional investors and board governance.

How long does the initial setup take? +

The Customer Data Cube build typically takes 4–8 weeks depending on data complexity. Your first board-ready ARR Snowball is delivered within that timeframe. Ongoing reporting is automated from that point forward.

What systems do you connect to? +

Salesforce, HubSpot, Stripe, Chargebee, Zuora, NetSuite, Sage, and any system with API or CSV export. We build on Microsoft Fabric, so your data stays within your Azure tenant.

How is this different from FP&A tools like Vena or Planful? +

FP&A tools are general-purpose budgeting platforms. Pacer AI is purpose-built for M&A-grade ARR analysis — account × product level granularity, multi-dimensional waterfall decomposition, and transaction-ready outputs that survive buy-side diligence.

What does pricing look like? +

The data cube build is priced at half a basis point of ARR (min $25k). Annual platform subscription is $70.2k per company. This is 60–85% less than a Big 4 engagement for the same M&A-grade output — and it’s ongoing, not a one-time sprint.

Do you work with PE funds directly? +

Yes. We work with Operating Partners and portfolio operations teams who need standardized ARR reporting across multiple portfolio companies. The portfolio tier provides a single pane of glass for ARR quality across your entire portfolio.
