Pricing questions

What comes with each agent?

Five users, documentation and Slack agent support. Additional users are $50 per user per month.

Annual or month-to-month?

Annual plans are billed yearly up front at the lower monthly rate. Month-to-month plans carry no annual commitment.

What does installation cover?

Each integration, such as your CRM or an ERP/EPM like NetSuite, is a one-time $3,500.

Can I try it first?

Yes. The Pacer AI demo runs in Claude on a synthetic company with fictional accounts. Add the connector and type pace.
