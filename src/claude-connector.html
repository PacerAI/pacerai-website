What Pacer AI does in Claude

Pacer AI is the Daily Pacing Agent for sales leaders. In Claude it answers from the demo company’s CRM data, with tables and charts in the chat:

  • Are we on pace? Bookings to date against the plan to date, for the month, the quarter and the year.
  • What is the gap? The target minus the forecast.
  • How do we close it? The renewals to defend, the expansion to add and the new customers to find, with the reps and accounts behind each.

The connector reads data and changes nothing. No real customer’s data is in the demo.

Terms used on this page. Plan: the company’s target for the period. Booked: closed-won bookings so far. Forecast: booked plus open pipeline weighted by its probability of closing. Pace: booked compared with the plan to date. Gap: target minus forecast. New Logo: ARR from new customers. Net Expansion: upgrades from existing customers, less downsell and churn. White space: the ARR an existing account would add by buying every product for all of the seats on its subscription.

Add it to Claude

  1. From Claude’s connector directory after the listing is approved: open claude.ai/directory/connectors/pacer-ai and click Connect.
  2. Or add it now as a custom connector. Signed in to claude.ai in your browser, the button opens Claude’s Add custom connector dialog with the name and server URL filled in. Click Continue, keep the defaults, then click Add.

  3. On a Claude Team or Enterprise plan, only an Owner can add a connector. Ask your Owner, or use a personal Claude account.

Sign in with your work email

Claude opens the Pacer AI sign-in page. Enter your work email, pass the quick bot check and click Start the demo. There is no password. If no sign-in page appears, open Settings → Connectors → Pacer AI - Demo and click Connect.

Optional: in the same settings, set Tool permissions to Always allow, so Claude draws each chart without stopping to ask.

Your email tells us who is using the demo, and we may follow up with you about Pacer AI. The privacy policy sets out what the connector receives and what we keep.

What you can ask Pacer

This list may change slightly after review.

Ask in plain English; Claude picks the command. Each command, and the question it answers:

CommandWhat it answers
paceAre we on pace for the month, the quarter and the year? Booked, plan to date, forecast and gap for each; the plan by motion (New Logo and Net Expansion); the reps behind the number; and the largest movements. Ask gap for the gap and the two ways to close it.
make_planWhat does it take to make plan this month, quarter or year? What is booked, what is lined up in the pipeline, what is still to find, and where to find it.
planWhat is the plan? ARR, growth and net new ARR by year, actual and planned, and how this year’s forecast moves the plan.
pace_quarterlyAre we making the quarter? Quarter-to-date bookings against the quarter’s target, with a forecast for the quarter end.
pace_annualWill we make the year? Year-to-date ARR against the plan line, the gap to the annual target and where the year lands.
top_moversWhat might we lose, what must we defend, and what can we add? The five largest churn risks, renewals and expansion deals still to come in the period.
rep_attainmentWho is carrying the number? Each rep’s quota, attainment to date and projected attainment, with the activity behind it.
rep_pipelineIs each rep’s pipeline enough? Coverage of the quota still to book, pipeline by stage, stage conversion and time in stage.
rep_leadsHow many leads is each rep getting, from which sources, and how many become deals?
renewalsWhich accounts were up for renewal in the period, and how did each renewal go?
new_bookingsWhich new-customer deals closed in the period: won, still open or lost?
new_logosWhere are the next new customers? Prospects that resemble current customers, and past champions who have moved to a prospect.
whitespace_listWhich accounts renewing in the period have the most room to expand, ranked by white space?
whitespace_detailProduct by product, how much white space does each renewing account have?
account_productsWhat does an account own, product by product, in a given period?
arr_chart_annualWhat is our ARR, and how has it moved this year? ARR at each period end, with new-customer ARR shown on top.
arr_chart_quarterWhat is our ARR at each month end this quarter?
metricsWhich metrics can Pacer report, and how is each one defined?
dimensionsWhat can I cut the numbers by (market, segment, the year a customer joined), and how is ARR split across each?
demo_contextWhat company is this? What it sells, where and to whom, and what the labels Market A–C and Product A–C stand for.
data_qualityWhat is missing from the data, why, and whose job is it to fix it?
healthIs the demo running? Did last night’s update run, and is the morning briefing sending?
