## Assistant URL notes (November 2026)

- **Claude** — `https://claude.ai/new?q={prompt}`. Documented to prefill and wait for the person to send. Automated fetch of the origin returned a Cloudflare 403 challenge, so prefill was not re-checked in a browser this turn.
- **ChatGPT** — `https://chatgpt.com/?q={prompt}`. `?prompt=` is treated the same. Origin returned HTTP 200. Public docs (updated July 2026) say it fills the composer and does not auto-send from a third-party click.
- **Grok** — `https://grok.com/?q={prompt}`. Origin is reachable. Public docs say it often submits on load, or shows a "Send this message?" confirm, and needs sign-in. Treat a click as sending, not just offering, the prompt.
- **Copilot** — use `https://copilot.com/?q={prompt}`. A request to `https://copilot.microsoft.com/?q=...` 301s to `copilot.com` and keeps `q`. Prefill is the weak one: Microsoft Q&A (late 2025) says `?q=` may show as a heading rather than filling the chat box, and a 13 Nov 2026 logged-out test reported the prompt dropping on the sign-in redirect. Do not add `autorun=1`.
