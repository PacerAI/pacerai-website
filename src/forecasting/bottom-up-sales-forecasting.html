Questions

What is bottom-up sales forecasting?

Bottom-up sales forecasting builds the number from booked revenue and open pipeline at the account, product and rep, then reconciles it to Finance. Pacer AI is that agent for sales leaders. The forecast updates in real time.

How is a bottom-up forecast different from a roll-up?

A roll-up adds the numbers reps and managers submit. A bottom-up forecast starts from the accounts: what is booked, what is open, and which accounts can close the gap. Pacer AI builds the second kind, and reconciles it to Finance.

Who is Pacer AI for?

CROs, VP Sales, RevOps and CFOs at B2B companies with complex GTM motions and $10M or more in recurring revenue.
