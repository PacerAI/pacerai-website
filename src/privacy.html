Privacy policy

What Pacer AI collects on getpacerai.com, in the Pacer AI connector for Claude and in the emails we send; why we collect it; where it is kept; and how to have it corrected or deleted.

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE — Will]

In brief

  • We collect the work email you give us, a name and company if you add them, your IP address, and how you use the website and the connector.
  • We use it to give you the demo, answer your requests, follow up with you about Pacer AI, and see which pages and commands people use.
  • The connector receives only the commands Claude sends it. It never receives your conversation with Claude.
  • The demo runs on a synthetic company. Its customers, reps and deals are fictional.
  • We do not sell personal data.

In the Pacer AI connector for Claude

The connector is the Pacer AI demo you add to Claude: the HubSpot demo, or the Salesforce demo we give you on request. Both work the same way, and this section covers both. Each answers questions about pacing to plan from the CRM data of a synthetic company with fictional customers, reps and deals, held in Pacer AI’s own HubSpot or Salesforce account. That account holds no personal data about you.

What the connector receives. When Claude decides to use Pacer AI, it sends the connector a command name and its arguments, for example pace with grain: quarter. The connector does not receive your conversation, your other connectors’ data or files you share with Claude. The connector calls no AI model. Anthropic’s handling of your Claude conversations is covered by Anthropic’s privacy policy.

Emails we send

  • Follow-up emails after a sign-up, a download or a demo sign-in, sent through Apollo.io from a Pacer AI mailbox.
  • Order summaries after a pricing request, sent through Resend from orders@mail.getpacerai.com.
  • The demo’s daily pacing briefing. If you sign up for the demo, or we add you by name, we may email you the demo company’s daily pacing briefing through Resend from reports@mail.getpacerai.com. Demo sign-ups receive it on three days and then it stops. We keep a record of which addresses received which day’s briefing, on the Azure server.

To stop any of these, reply and ask us to stop, use the opt-out link where an email has one, or write to support@getpacerai.com.

  • correct it;

Where EU or UK data-protection law applies, we rely on your request (to give you the demo or answer you) and on our legitimate interest in following up with business contacts who came to us. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, including the right to complain to your data-protection authority.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Access to our databases and logs is limited to Pacer’s team. Keys and passwords are kept in Azure Key Vault and Cloudflare’s secret storage, not in code. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you as the law requires.

Children

Pacer AI is a business product and is not meant for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us data, email support@getpacerai.com and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will post any change on this page with a new effective date. If a change affects how we use data you have already given us, we will say so at the top of this page.
