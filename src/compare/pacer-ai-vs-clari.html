Questions

Is Clari still a separate company?

No. Salesloft's merger page says Clari and Salesloft now operate as one company, with Salesloft as the company name, and that Clari Forecast keeps the Clari name. In Nov 2026, clari.com returned a 301 to https://www.salesloft.com/.

What is the forecasting difference?

Pacer AI builds the forecast from booked revenue and open pipeline at the account, product and rep, reconciles it to Finance, and updates it in real time. Clari Forecast is the forecasting product Salesloft still sells under the Clari name. Salesloft's public pages reviewed in Nov 2026 do not publish the arithmetic behind that forecast, so this page does not invent it. The question to ask is whether your number starts from a submitted roll-up or from the accounts.

Does Pacer publish a price Clari does not?

Pacer publishes seat prices: Rep pacing $100, Manager pacing $500, and SLT pacing and planning $1,000 per user a month, plus a $5,000 SLT installation and services from $15,000. This page does not state a Clari or Salesloft price, because the pages reviewed in Nov 2026 did not publish one.
