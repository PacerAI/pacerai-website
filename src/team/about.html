After watching the same pattern repeat across dozens of companies — smart teams burning months to produce basic revenue analytics that should already exist — he built Pacer AI. Over $25B+ in SaaS M&A transactions. 40+ customer data cubes built for advanced GTM sales planning, ARR forecasting, and operationalized for weekly Sales & RevOps cadences. West Point graduate.
