The Challenge

Why do boards of recurring revenue companies need more than a basic ARR Waterfall?

Boards and leadership teams of recurring revenue businesses need to know expansion rate and the drivers of expansion and contraction in order to improve the durable revenue growth of the business.

A basic ARR Waterfall (one period displayed only) or a basic ARR Snowball (multiple periods displayed using an ARR Waterfall format) doesn’t provide upsell and cross-sell visibility or the percentage of the ARR movement broken out.

First-Time Build

What should a board-quality ARR Snowball analysis look like?

A board-quality ARR Snowball should show cross-sell and upsell along with product churn and downsell in order to understand the drivers of expansion and contraction. Additionally, management teams and the board need to be able to see under the hood on Churn to see how much ARR was up for renewal, and how much churned. The same “double-click” is needed to understand how much New ARR is from customer winbacks (returning >12 mo’s) and poor renewal process (Lapsed <12 mo’s). The percentage analysis of ARR as a % of Beginning makes the ARR Snowball easier to digest so users can quickly see churn rates and upsell rates.

Clearly an ARR Snowball of this granularity and supporting information wouldn’t go on a slide, but the analysis to explain the ARR growth drivers and the churn drivers is what ARR Snowballs are built for.

Beyond Reporting

Are ARR Snowballs only used for reporting?

No, building an ARR Snowball requires data engineering and data modeling which allows analytics professionals to calculate and display whitespace by account (upsell and cross-sell opportunities), by market (aggregate of account whitespace) and by key account (subsidiary accounts of a global account to sell to).

The architecture required to build ARR Snowballs also allows analytics professionals to create multi-dimensional cohort views to see where growth is accelerating and why. Which products drive cross-sell. Which account sizes drive NRR and many more views.
